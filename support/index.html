Support

Found a bug or want something? Tell us: feedback@onlatravel.com

The fastest route is the Send feedback row in the app's You tab — it pre-fills your app version so we can help without the back-and-forth.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Onla works fully offline with no account. Signing in adds backup and sync across devices — nothing else.

How do I import a booking? Forward the confirmation email to your import address (shown in the You tab), or photograph the confirmation from the ＋ button. Everything waits for your review before it touches a trip.

How do I get my data out? You tab → Export all data. It's yours.
